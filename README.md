
# Smart Inventory Management AI

## Summary

Smart Inventory Management AI is a proposed system that uses artificial intelligence to help small businesses monitor inventory, predict product demand, and avoid overstocking or running out of products. This is a Building AI course project.

## Background

Many small businesses struggle to manage their inventory. They may purchase too many products or run out of popular items. This project aims to help business owners make better inventory decisions using AI.

## How is it used?

Business owners enter their product names, available stock, and sales records. The system analyzes the information and predicts which products may need restocking.

## Data sources and AI methods

The system can use historical sales records, product quantities, and purchasing patterns. Machine learning methods such as regression and classification can help predict product demand and identify low-stock products.

## Challenges

The accuracy of predictions depends on the quality and quantity of sales data. Small businesses may also have limited access to digital records. Customer privacy and data security must be considered.

## What next?

In the future, the project could include a working application, automatic stock alerts, sales dashboards, and more accurate demand predictions.
